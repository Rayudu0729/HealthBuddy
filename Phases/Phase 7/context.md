# Phase 7 — Compact wearable integration

Planned: 13 November 2026 (Asia/Calcutta). Status: pending; no completion evidence recorded.

Turn the validated bench prototype into a battery-powered wrist device. Work is planned; final dimensions, PCB, battery design and runtime remain unvalidated.

## Requirements and work

- [ ] Measure idle, sensing, vibration and LTE transmit consumption.
- [ ] Select protected LiPo dimensions/capacity and peak-current capability.
- [ ] Design charging, protection, power-path and regulators together.
- [ ] Finalise sensor orientation, modem, antenna clearance and GPIO allocation.
- [ ] Reserve accessible SOS and cancel/test controls; review the final-phase movement-check button mapping.
- [ ] Produce an electrical/mechanical layout and compact PCB design.
- [ ] Fabricate, assemble and test the PCB.
- [ ] Build an insulated case and adjustable strap.
- [ ] Measure dimensions, runtime, heat, charging behaviour and radio reception.
- [ ] Recheck local alerts and end-to-end LTE delivery after enclosure integration.

## Completion evidence

Demonstrate a self-contained battery-powered wrist prototype with measured fit/runtime and successful SOS delivery over LTE. Record enclosure effects on distance sensing and radio reception. Review the effect of the planned CSI receive mode on power before claiming runtime for that mode.

No waterproofing, clinical accuracy or production-readiness claim is established.
